Use the operational centre around traffic, protected assets and actual approaches. Put outer defences between likely incursions and protected sites. Keep freighter and L/XL approaches open. The network needs real commissioning supplies and external or supplemental inputs. The source model also flags the initial Protein Paste deficit and short Computronic raw-storage cover. Confirm actual consumption, workforce scaling, storage and routes in a disposable save before balancing the blueprints.

## Construction and servicing

A secured sector receives a central Administration Base project and thirteen concurrent infrastructure projects. A project waits independently for a buildable site, real resources, a Builder or other access it lacks. There is no artificial per-sector concurrency cap. Use the native construction queue, build storage and Builder deployment.

A provisional Administration Base provides ownership, local command, docks and forward staging. The test design adds modest storage and S/M maintenance capability, whose actual service behavior must be checked in game. Larger repairs and fleet rearming rely on capable High Commands and Honshus. A selected Administration Base can expand into High Command through the native build process. Final player-authored geometry, exact defence equipment and completed-station decommissioning remain open.

## Arsenal and front gate

- One **Arsenal** sector can concentrate industry, logistics, reserves, maintenance and heavy staging, up to 40 permanent stations. Nopileos' Fortune is the preferred candidate subject to placement and claim feasibility. The Arsenal has no fabrication facility.
- The accelerator-connected **front gate** is its own operational sector with normal security, stronger static gateway defence and mobile Honshu support as needed. It omits the standard industrial package. The associated High Command handles fixed regional servicing, including refit where supported.

## Procurement

The founding Kyushu Builder is the one direct ship-creation exception. YoRHa has a 14-Builder ceiling. Builders #2–14, ordinary ships and replacements use paid, resource-backed orders at eligible player yards. If zero operational Builders remain and no eligible player yard can supply one, a single paid Terran Builder order is allowed for continuity.

The A0.4 engineering record uses native requested-job tickets with zero standing population, checks yard eligibility and blueprint access, uses native yard prices and queues, and waits for native completion before assigning the hull. Keep one durable identity per procurement slot. On reload, reconcile the request, yard and resulting ship before retrying. A busy yard or missing materials is a visible wait state. YoRHa has no wharf or shipyard of its own and ordinary shortages never generate free ships or wares.

Demand-driven construction traders, station traders and mining capacity support actual shortages. Prefer native station managers, trade/mining orders and current Terran production recipes.
