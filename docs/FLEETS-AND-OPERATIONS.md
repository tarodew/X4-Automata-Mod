Reserve Nimchas are available individually for police vacancies, partial wing losses, complete five-ship wings or temporary reinforcement. Permanent formations keep their approved establishments and report actual shortages. The Tokyo's fighters screen and intercept. The older bomber-wing concept is retired.

## Incident handling

Police handle scans, compliance and local offenders under applicable jurisdiction. Normal combat response uses two five-Nimcha wings, with reinforcements added in five-ship increments. One wing can make first contact while backup is assigned. Coverage follows traffic, gates, industrial clusters and observed incidents.

For an attacked player trader or miner, the response can cover a temporary retreat and restore its prior assignment once safe. Outside protected space, escort to the next safe gate or equivalent handoff. This automatic rescue scope covers player civilian ships.

Escalate as needed from sector patrol to regional capital response and High Command heavy defence, then to Rapid Response for urgent reinforcement, regular War Divisions for sustained operations, or the Asgard division for exceptional threats. A known severe incident can go directly to the appropriate layer. Avoid stripping regional defenders or Builder escorts for routine offensive assignments.

## Readiness, service and replacement

- Keep one stable identity per formation, incident, operation and deficit. A reservation prevents a ship, Builder or formation from accepting conflicting assignments.
- Mark a force ready from observed membership, commander, equipment, damage, service and regrouping. A timeout triggers reassessment. It never confirms readiness.
- Recall deployed groups, verify docking or regrouping, service them, reconcile losses and return them to duty. High Commands service assigned regional and visiting forces. War Divisions can use organic Honshus in theatre or a nearby High Command. Honshus provide mobile repair, rearm and resupply. Equipment refits use capable fixed facilities.
- Arsenal is the deep reconstitution point. Routine regional repairs stay local. A real deficit permits one outstanding paid request or reserved replacement. The replacement reaches the assigned ship or formation before a healthy Builder escort is released.
- Persist assignments, readiness and outstanding work across reload. Reconcile native state before issuing another order. Prefer native flight, orders, docking, combat and servicing. Use event-driven or bounded observations for faction decisions.

## Operation state

Record incident location, protected asset, observed contact, urgency, assigned response, losses, and the reason for closure. The expected progression is classification, reservation, dispatch, engagement, recovery and closure, with reinforcement or withdrawal when needed. Close only once the asset is safe or lost, pressure has ended or been handed off, and outstanding service and replacement work is recorded.

Strategic operations have their own identities and priorities. Construction queues expose site, build storage, Builder, materials, progress and completion. Procurement reports yard and equipment access, material and queue waits, delivery, assignment and verification. The player report should distinguish an accepted directive from a completed result.
